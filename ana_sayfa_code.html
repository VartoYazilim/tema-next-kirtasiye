Est. 2018

Crafted for the creators, the thinkers, and the dreamers.

The Atelier began in a small apartment in Kyoto with a simple observation: the digital world is fast, but the human heart is tactile. We believe that putting pen to paper is an act of mindfulness.

Every product in our collection is hand-selected or custom-designed to provide a sensory experience that inspires your best work. We source from family-owned mills and artisan workshops who share our commitment to quality and sustainability.
